Case Study & Background
Case Study
–
A Client Advisor at Marsh wants to accelerate the creation 
of client-specific marketing pitches for insurance policies.
–
You are hired as a consultant to build a UI-driven, AI-
powered application.
–
The application generates a tailored marketing pitch 
presentation for any client company.
–
The pitch must be grounded in the 4 medical policy 
documents shared with you.
Background
–
Advisors spend significant time manually researching client 
companies and curating policy content for pitches.
–
Policy documents outline coverage, benefits, exclusions 
and pricing that must be accurately reflected.
–
Inaccurate or unsupported coverage claims create 
commercial and compliance risk for the firm.
–
A tool is needed where an advisor inputs a company name 
and instantly gets an accurate, well-structured pitch 
grounded in policy documents — with confidence the AI 
content has been checked.

Challenge 1: Build the AI-Powered Pitch Generator
Develop a UI-driven application that generates a client-specific marketing pitch for Marsh’s insurance 
policies.
●
Objective 1.1 — UI: input field for company name, ability to select/upload policy document(s) as baseline, and a generate 
button.
●
Objective 1.2 — generateCompanyProfile(company_name): gathers company info (industry, size, key risks); falls back to 
clearly-labelled assumptions if data is unavailable.
●
Objective 1.3 — generateMarketingPitch(): produces a 3–5 slide deck covering company overview, why choose Marsh, 
policy benefits mapped to exposures, and one final recommended policy.
●
Objective 1.4 — input validation and error handling for missing company name, missing documents, or generation failures.

Challenge 2: Audit the AI-Generated Content
AI-generated pitch content carries a risk of inaccurate, unsupported or fabricated claims about policy 
coverage ("hallucination").
●
Before any pitch reaches a client, the firm needs confidence every benefit and figure is grounded in the source policy 
documents.
●
Objective 2.1 — build an auditing layer that traces each claim to a specific policy clause and flags untraceable statements 
for human review.
●
Objective 2.1 (cont.) — produce an audit summary (e.g. confidence score or pass/fail flag) alongside the generated deck.
●
Objective 2.2 — implement auditPitchContent(pitch_slides, policy_docs) returning a structured audit report, and describe 
how the advisor uses it to approve, edit, or reject the pitch.
